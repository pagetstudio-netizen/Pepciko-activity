The daily login check-in pays according to current VIP level every 24 hours to the withdrawal balance, only while the user has at least one active stable product: VIP1 45, VIP2 60, VIP3 75, VIP4 90, VIP5 105, VIP6 120, VIP7 135, and VIP8+ 150 FCFA. VIP level is based on cumulative approved deposits. A missed local calendar day resets the consecutive-day streak; previously credited funds and lifetime cumulative earnings remain.

The account’s « Récompenses » page shows the cumulative total and history of daily check-in credits in FCFA; keep unrelated reward types out of this history.

For the hero, use a crop from the user's supplied check-in reference screenshot, preserving its English text and original chevron. Do not redraw the artwork or translate text embedded in the image; keep the back action clickable over the chevron.

**Why:** The user approved a VIP-scaled reward schedule, retained the daily check-in and missed-day rules, requested the check-in history on the account’s « Récompenses » page, and corrected a generated hero.

**How to apply:** Calculate the reward from the member’s current approved-deposit VIP level on the server; never accept the amount from the browser. Verify active stable-product eligibility before crediting, and calculate the streak from local claim dates without clawing back funds. On the « Récompenses » page, total and list only daily check-in credits. Keep surrounding interface text in French and FCFA.

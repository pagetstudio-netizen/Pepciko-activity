---
name: Team investment statistics
description: Which products count as purchases and investment in member-facing team statistics.
---

On the member-facing Team page, an administrator-assigned product must not count as a referral's purchase, active purchase, or invested amount. Count only positive, paid, user-made product purchases; use the recorded purchase price for investment totals.

**Why:** The user explicitly said an Admin-assigned product must not make a sponsor's referral look as if they bought or invested in a product.

**How to apply:** Keep these member-facing statistics distinct from withdrawal eligibility and the Admin's product-assignment view unless the user asks to change those rules too.

On the Compte tab, make the VIP, business/activity, and other-services cards slightly wider than the narrowed version, while keeping them shorter vertically. Do not narrow these cards to save height.

**Why:** The user repeatedly corrected changes that made the Compte cards too narrow; they want a small width increase together with reduced height.

**How to apply:** Preserve the account shell’s horizontal padding and widen the cards themselves slightly. Reduce card heights, vertical padding, and gaps without changing unrelated account layout.

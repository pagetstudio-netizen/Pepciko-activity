---
name: Product payout policy
description: The agreed earnings policy for purchased stable, activity, and wellness products.
---

Stable, activity, and wellness products accrue the configured daily amount for calculation, but pay gains only once at the end of the product period. On investment cards, stable products show their daily amount under “Gains quotidiens”; wellness products show the daily amount under “Gains journaliers” and display purchase price plus full-cycle profit as “Revenu total”; activity products show the full-period return under “Gains à l’échéance”. These display choices do not change payout timing or the maturity payout rules. Existing positions remain active and transition to this policy; any gains already credited before the transition count toward the promised total and must be deducted from the maturity payment. Free daily check-in bonuses are separate from product earnings.

**Why:** the user specified that wellness cards should show daily gains and that their displayed total revenue includes the purchase price plus profit, while the established maturity payout policy remains unchanged.

**How to apply:** never credit purchased-product earnings through a daily or manual-claim path. On maturity, atomically complete the position, credit only the unpaid remainder, and create at most one final earning transaction.
